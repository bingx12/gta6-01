**VELDORA BAY** — an original open-world crime game. Rae Calloway, ex-getaway driver, is back in a sun-soaked coastal city and owes the fixer Marla Voss one last job. Steal cars, outrun the VBPD, run five story missions, and take the casino cash in the final heist.

What is in the game:

- A 3D open world built with three.js: a street grid of procedural towers with lit windows, palms, streetlights, a beach and ocean at sunset, fog and long shadows.
- On foot and by car: WASD movement with a third-person orbit camera, jumping, and arcade driving with steering, collisions and car stealing (E).
- Traffic cars that cruise the grid and turn at intersections, pedestrians who stroll the sidewalks and flee from danger.
- A wanted system (0–5 stars): stealing, hit-and-runs and shootouts raise the heat; police cars and foot cops chase, ram and bust you; lie low and the heat cools off.
- Five story missions with dialogue, objectives, map markers and cash rewards: a tutorial drive, a getaway chase, a timed parcel run, a dockside shootout, and the final armored-van heist.
- Combat: a pistol with mouse aim, tracers and muzzle flash, armed enemies with health bars who shoot back, player health with regen, WASTED and BUSTED screens with respawn.
- A full HUD: minimap with player, traffic, cops and mission marker, health, cash, wanted stars, speed, in-game clock, objective tracker and mission list.
- Menus (start / pause / respawn), save and continue via localStorage, mute toggle, and fully procedural WebAudio: engine hum, sirens, gunshots, pickup blips and a synth music loop. No external assets — everything runs from these files, zero network requests.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Click START, and press M to begin the first mission. Screenshot scenes are staged with `?shot=start|drive|chase|mission&seed=1`.
